The Rosetta Stone, discovered in 1799 by French soldiers in Egypt, was the key to deciphering ancient Egyptian hieroglyphs. The stone contains the same text written in three scripts: Ancient Greek, Demotic, and Egyptian hieroglyphs. Because scholars could already read Ancient Greek, Jean-François Champollion successfully deciphered hieroglyphs in 1822, unlocking thousands of years of Egyptian history that had been unreadable for centuries.
